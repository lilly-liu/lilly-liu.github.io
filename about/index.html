About

Curious about systems, and the people who use them.

I'm Lilly — a software engineer who cares as much about how a tool feels as what it does.

I studied Data Science and Economics at UC Berkeley, where I got hooked on the space between data, decisions, and design. That mix still shapes how I work: I like problems that are technical and human at the same time.

Today I build AI-native tools at Cambridge Mobile Telematics — platforms for internal chatbots and workflow assistants, safe self-serve data access, incident response, and developer tooling that raises the floor for everyone on the team.

I care about making complex systems feel simple. The best engineering I've seen disappears into a clear interface, a fast feedback loop, or a decision someone can finally make with confidence.

Engineering philosophy

Build the simplest thing that makes a hard decision easy — then make it feel good to use.
