Hey! I’m Lilly.

Programmer, product-manager-in-training, and musician.

I love working on AI systems and making AI approachable and usable for others. While AI makes building things faster and easier, I’m even more interested in tech and social good: how can we make society better—or even just more fun—with all these things we can do now?

At Cambridge Mobile Telematics, I’m building projects that make people’s lives easier.

In my free time, I’m building hobbyBFF, an app where people can meet and learn new things together. It grew out of moving to Boston: I met some of my best friends after college by bonding over not knowing how to do something, and I want everyone to be so lucky.

I’m a big fan of Spotify playlists, musicals, playing trivia, and funky wine bottle holders. You can catch me at AMC with my friends or at the boxing gym on weekends.
